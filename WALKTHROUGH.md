# Neon Pong V9: what's new, a walkthrough

A guided tour of everything added since V8. Short on theory, long on "press this and see".

---

## 1. Your first 2 minutes

1. **Open the game.** A lazy demo rally plays behind the menu. Pick a racket: each card now shows **speed, power, spin, agility and reach** bars.
2. **Daily reward pops up.** Press **CLAIM**. Come back every day: the 7-day calendar pays 🪙25 → 150, and **day 7 gives a free pack**. Miss a day and the streak restarts. Click your coin pill any time to see it.
3. Press **Enter** (or the red button) to play the AI. Your racket, score target, deuce rule and difficulty are remembered next time.

## 2. Playing a match

| Do this | Result |
|---|---|
| Move (mouse, **WASD**, arrows, touch, controller stick) | Bat chases your hand. Light rackets follow instantly, the Titan feels heavy. |
| **Space** / click | Strike: lunge forward. Time it on a fast ball for a **Perfect Parry**. |
| **Shift** / right-click | Chrono Dash (50 Overdrive; Phantom only 35). |
| **1-8** or the 😎 button | Emotes. |
| **Z / X** | Fire your two equipped **superpowers**. |
| **P** or **Esc** | Pause (vs AI). The game also auto-pauses if you alt-tab. |
| **H** | Full shortcut list. |

**Serving:** use **Q / E** (or `[` `]`, the mouse wheel, or the on-screen AIM buttons) to aim. The dotted line previews the **wall bounce**, and the faint cone shows the small random wobble every serve has.

**Where the ball meets the blade matters**
- Dead-centre = **🎯 Sweetspot** (more speed).
- Clipping the tip = **🔪 Edge cut** (slower, with spin).
- A tilted blade angles your return.

**Over the net:** floating pickups appear at rally 3, 9 and 16: 🎰 2X Fever, 🪞 Mirage, 💰 Coin Burst (+30 🪙) and ⏱ Time Warp. The net ripples when the ball crosses.

**Match point:** a gold vignette pulses, and on the shot that could end the match time slows down (**🎬 LAST POINT**). Winning gets a short "GAME!" moment, and confetti.

## 4. Coins, packs and the locker (**L**)

- **Earn coins:** match wins (and losses a little), bounties, 💰 pickups, daily rewards, friend stakes.
- **Packs:** 🥉 Rookie 🪙90 (1 item) • 🥈 Pro 🪙220 (2 items, 1 Epic+) • 🏆 Legend 🪙520 (3 items, Epic+). Packs favour items you don't own yet; duplicates refund coins.
- **30 cosmetics:** ball skins, net styles (laser, lightning, orbit drones), trails, courts and **6 racket skins** (Chrome, Inferno, Permafrost, Midas Gold, Glitch, Aurora Prism). Your opponent sees your racket skin online.

## 5. Superpowers ⚡ (Locker → POWERS tab)

Buy **charges** with coins (up to 9 each), equip two into slots **Z** and **X**, use them mid-match. Each slot works **once per match**, 4 seconds apart. Everyone starts with a free 🛡️ and ⚡.

| Power | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ Goal Guard | 60 | Saves one point |
| 🧊 Ice Lock | 70 | Opponent's bat in slow motion for 2.5 s |
| 🧲 Magnet Hand | 50 | Ball is pulled to your bat for 5 s |
| ⚡ Overload | 80 | Instantly full Overdrive |
| 🪞 Mirage Clone | 45 | Decoy ball |
| 🌫️ Fog of War | 65 | Opponent can barely see the ball on their side for 4 s |

Powers are **disabled in ranked matches**. You can see your rival's equipped powers in the bar, so bluffing is part of the game.

## 6. Playing a friend

1. **Invite Friend** → share the link. Phones, tablets and PCs all work, and everyone sees the court from their own side.
2. Optional **Friend Stakes** (🪙25 / 50 / 100, both players pay). Winner takes the pot. Leaving mid-match forfeits it.
3. After each match you see your **head-to-head record** ("⚔️ vs Alex: 3-2") and can **Rematch** (**R** / Enter).
4. Check the ping colour (green / yellow / red). If it's red, use Wi-Fi.

## 7. Phones, tablets, controllers

- Touch: trackpad / joystick / buttons / court-drag modes (Controls, **C**). AIM and power buttons appear on screen.
- **Controller:** stick moves • A strike • X dash • LB/RB aim • LT/RT powers • Start pause.
- **Offline:** after one visit the game works vs the AI with no connection.
- Reduced-motion devices skip screen shake, flashes and confetti. If the game ever drops below ~47 fps it switches itself to LITE graphics.

## 8. Smaller things worth knowing

- Press **H** for every keyboard shortcut. Header buttons show their keys.
- Green dots mark "something to do": an unclaimed daily reward, or enough coins for a pack.
- The AI plays like a person now: it reacts late, misreads some shots, and its strength is the same on 60 Hz and 144 Hz screens.
